How grading works

Email grades

Every email is graded. A guess is never labeled verified.

  1. A

    Published by the business, next to the person’s name.

  2. Verified

    Mailbox confirmed by an email-verification check.

  3. Likely

    The mailbox exists, but couldn’t be firmly confirmed.

  4. Business inbox

    An address like info@. At small spas, usually read by the owner.

  5. Left out

    If we can’t check an address, it isn’t in your list.
